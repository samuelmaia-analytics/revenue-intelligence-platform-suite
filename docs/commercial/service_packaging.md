# Service Packaging

## Packaging Matrix
| Package | Best fit | Core promise |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue Command Center | recurring-revenue operators | one executive operating view for revenue protection |
| Retention Intelligence | CS and retention teams | proactive churn defense with action prioritization |
| Executive KPI Governance | executive leadership | trusted KPI system with ownership and freshness |
| Commercial Performance Analytics | sales and commercial ops | sharper performance visibility without dashboard sprawl |

## Revenue Command Center
- Best fit: recurring-revenue businesses with fragmented commercial and customer signals.
- Core deliverables: executive KPI board, account prioritization, action register, telemetry instrumentation.
- Primary value: create one operating view for weekly executive revenue management.

## Retention Intelligence
- Best fit: customer success and retention teams that need proactive churn defense.
- Core deliverables: risk scoring, segment drill-downs, save-motion recommendations, adoption monitoring.
- Primary value: move from reactive churn response to prioritized intervention.

## Executive KPI Governance
- Best fit: leadership teams that need trust, consistency, and accountability in metrics.
- Core deliverables: KPI definitions, freshness controls, scorecards, owner model, governance workflow.
- Primary value: turn KPI reporting into a governed executive system.

## Commercial Performance Analytics
- Best fit: sales and commercial operations teams focused on efficiency and leakage reduction.
- Core deliverables: performance analytics, opportunity mapping, executive review pack, action prioritization.
- Primary value: improve commercial decision quality without expanding dashboard sprawl.

## Delivery Modes
- advisory plus build
- accelerator implementation
- recurring analytics managed service
- white-label platform foundation
