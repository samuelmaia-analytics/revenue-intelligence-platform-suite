# Executive Brief

## Executive Summary
Revenue Intelligence Platform Suite unifies revenue, retention, and commercial decision support into one executive operating layer. The objective is to reduce revenue exposure, improve decision speed, and create a governed cadence for action and accountability.

## Strategic Objectives
- protect revenue at risk through value-based prioritization
- improve allocation of commercial and retention effort
- reduce time from raw signal to executive action
- create repeatable proof for recurring analytics service delivery

## Business Scope
- revenue intelligence
- retention and churn exposure
- commercial performance analytics
- executive KPI governance
- executive decision support

## Executive Decision Layer
- current performance versus target
- value at risk by account, segment, or motion
- prioritized recommended actions with expected recovery
- scenario comparison for conservative, base, and aggressive plans
- confidence signals for quality, freshness, and platform health

## North Star KPIs
- Net Revenue Retention
- Gross Revenue Churn
- Revenue at Risk
- Action Adoption Rate
- Time-to-Insight
- Data Quality Score

## Operating Model
- Weekly Business Review: performance, exposure, and action progression
- Monthly Steering Review: ownership, quality, and roadmap alignment
- Quarterly Executive Review: realized delta, proof pack, and scope expansion

## Governance
- versioned contracts for executive outputs
- quality and freshness scorecards
- CI-backed validation and artifact gates
- traceable insight generation and release evidence

## 12-Month Outcome Targets Template
- reduce revenue churn by `__%`
- recover incremental value of `__$`
- reduce time-to-insight by `__%`
- increase recommendation adoption to `__%`

## Key Risks And Mitigations
- inconsistent source quality
  mitigation: contracts, scorecards, and promotion gates
- low commercial adoption of recommendations
  mitigation: owner mapping, action telemetry, and playbook review
- performance drift or KPI trust erosion
  mitigation: monitoring, scorecards, and release evidence cadence

- platform baseline: complete
- client asset generation: complete
- deal-room and GTM enablement: complete
